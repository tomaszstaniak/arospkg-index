NAME
     Sploiner 1.01 (Aug 18 2025 11:17:04) - Split, Join & Repair.
     Splits large files into smaller parts for transportation 
     on disk. Possibility for error recovery.

SYNOPSIS
     Sploiner split  <InputFile> [-n] [-s Disksize] [-o Outputname]

     Sploiner join <basename> as <joined_file>

     Sploiner repair file_1 file_2 ... file_n as damaged_file

     Sploiner example

DISCLAIMER
     I take NO responsibilities for this program.

DESCRIPTION
     Splits a program op in smaller sizes, recovers a lost file, 
     or join files.

     Type "Sploiner split", "Sploiner join" or "Sploiner repair"
     for detailed description and "Sploiner example" for an example.

OPTIONS
     split <file> [-n] [-s <size>] [-o <name>]
          Split a file.

     join <basename> as <file>
          Join <basename>.nnn and save as <file>

     repair <files> as <file>
          Cast spell to summon a lost file.

     example
          Gives a detailed example of sploiner usage.
